# Pitch Deck (Content Outline)

## Slide 1 — Title
**FoodLink** – AI recipe & nutrition recommender  
Tagline: *Turn what's in your kitchen into meals.*  
Team: [Names]

## Slide 2 — Problem (Zero Hunger)
- Households face food insecurity & nutrition gaps
- Food waste from unused ingredients
- Current solutions are generic, not local or budget-aware

## Slide 3 — Solution
- Paste or select ingredients → 3 nutritious, low-cost recipes
- Nutrition score visible; missing items listed
- One-click purchase (IntaSend-ready)

## Slide 4 — How It Works
- Frontend: HTML/CSS/JS
- Backend: Flask (Python)
- AI: OpenAI (fallback to curated recipes for offline demo)
- Data: JSON now, Supabase-ready
- Payments: IntaSend (stubbed checkout)

## Slide 5 — Demo
- Live flow: input → recipes → (optional) checkout

## Slide 6 — Impact & Market
- Reduces waste; improves nutrition habits
- Target: low-income households, students, NGOs, school feeding
- Monetization: affiliate links to local markets, small checkout fees, premium plans

## Slide 7 — Security & Performance
- No PII required; minimal data
- Graceful fallbacks when offline
- Simple architecture → fast and resilient

## Slide 8 — Roadmap
- Local language support
- Price-aware recipes by location
- Supplier marketplace integration
- Basic nutrition calculator per serving

## Slide 9 — Team & Ask
- Who we are
- What we need post-hackathon (mentors, data partners, market pilots)
